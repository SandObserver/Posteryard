Clean, consistent posters for your whole media library.

Studio posters come in every font, tagline and layout. Posteryard swaps them for textless art with the title in the same spot on every poster, then renders, uploads and refreshes them on its own in Plex or Jellyfin as your library grows.

Settings

{{settings_count}} optional settings for badges, status labels, collections, Jellyfin, Maintainerr, alerts and the schedule. Commands, Plex labels and monitoring are in the full documentation.

Use Jellyfin instead of Plex

Replace PLEX_URL and PLEX_TOKEN with these two lines.

environment
- JELLYFIN_URL=http://192.168.1.10:8096
- JELLYFIN_API_KEY=your-jellyfin-api-key

Get alerts in Discord

Use the two parts of your Discord webhook URL. Any Apprise address works, such as ntfys:// or mailtos://.

environment
- NOTIFY_URLS=discord://WEBHOOK_ID/WEBHOOK_TOKEN

Count down to Maintainerr deletions

Titles Maintainerr will delete get LEAVING IN 5 DAYS, then TOMORROW, then TODAY.

environment
- MAINTAINERR_URL=http://192.168.1.10:6246

Streaming services outside the US

The default is US. Put your country first and keep US as a fallback. This example is the UK.

environment
- STREAMING_REGIONS=GB,US

Badges for 1080p and 5.1 too

By default only 4K, HDR10 or better, and Dolby Atmos get a badge.

environment
- QUALITY_MIN_VIDEO=1080
- QUALITY_MIN_AUDIO=5.1

Use Apple TV art where it exists

Titles Apple TV does not have keep TMDB art.

environment
- APPLE_ART=true

Go live on two titles first

posteryard find NAME lists rating keys. Remove ONLY_RATING_KEYS when you are happy.

environment
- DRY_RUN=false
- ONLY_RATING_KEYS=5646,8730

Show the episode title on thumbnails

Adds EPISODE N and the title to each episode still.

environment
- EPISODE_THUMBNAILS=titled
