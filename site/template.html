Clean, consistent posters for your whole Plex library.

Studio posters come in every font, tagline and layout. Posteryard swaps them for clean art with the title in the same spot on every poster, then renders, uploads and refreshes them on its own as your library grows.

Every title in the same place

Official posters mix fonts, taglines and clutter, and the title is often hard to read. Posteryard picks clean art and puts the title logo in one spot, at one size, over a soft fade. A shelf of posters finally looks like one library.

Quality at a glance

4K, HDR, Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos marks come from Plex's own analysis of the file. Choose the lowest level that earns a badge, or turn each one off.

QUALITY_MIN_VIDEO=2160QUALITY_MIN_HDR=hdr10QUALITY_MIN_AUDIO=atmos

Know what is leaving

Connect Maintainerr and titles it is about to remove say so on the poster, such as "Leaving in 3 days". Nobody misses a movie because it vanished overnight.
