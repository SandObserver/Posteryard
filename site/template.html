#ABFLOST

Plex draws the unwatched count top right. Posteryard keeps the streaming service mark top left, clear of it.

One look for the whole library

Official posters each have their own type, layout and clutter. Posteryard picks clean art and puts every title in the same spot.

Hands off

New titles get posters from the Plex webhook or the 15 minute sweep. A daily pass keeps the whole library current. Unchanged posters are not uploaded again.

Badges you control

4K, Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos marks come from Plex's media analysis. Set the minimum for each, or turn them off with off.

Your picks win

Switch art with a Plex label or a command. Posters you change by hand in Plex are left alone.
